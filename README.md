# Music Store SQL Analysis

A SQL-based data analysis project using the **Chinook digital music store database** to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, music preferences, and revenue patterns.

## Business Problem

The business wanted to understand:

* Which customers generate the highest revenue
* Which countries and cities contribute the most sales
* Which genres and artists are most popular
* How customer spending varies across countries and artists
* Which tracks have longer-than-average playing times
* Which customers and genres represent high-value opportunities

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer & Revenue Analysis

* Identified the senior-most employee
* Analyzed invoice volume by country
* Identified the highest-value invoices
* Determined the city generating the highest revenue
* Ranked customers by total spending

### 2. Music Preference & Genre Analysis

* Identified customers who purchased Rock music
* Ranked the top 10 Rock artists by number of tracks
* Identified tracks with above-average song length
* Analyzed customer purchasing behavior across genres

### 3. Advanced Business Intelligence Analysis

* Calculated customer spending by artist
* Identified the most popular genre for each country
* Determined the highest-spending customer within each country
* Compared customer and purchasing patterns across geographic markets

## SQL Techniques Used

* INNER JOIN / LEFT JOIN
* GROUP BY and HAVING
* Aggregate functions: `SUM()`, `COUNT()`, `AVG()`, `MAX()`
* Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
* Subqueries
* Window functions / ranking
* Filtering and sorting
* Multi-table relational analysis

## Business Insights

The analysis enabled identification of:

* High-value customers for targeted marketing
* High-revenue countries and cities
* Popular genres and artists
* Country-specific music preferences
* Customer spending patterns
* Tracks performing above the average song-length benchmark

These insights can support **customer segmentation, targeted marketing, artist partnerships, geographic event planning, and revenue optimization**.

## Tech Stack

**SQL | PostgreSQL | SQLite | Chinook Database**

**Core Skills:** SQL Joins, CTEs, Aggregations, Subqueries, Window Functions, Data Analysis, Business Intelligence
